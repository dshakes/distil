How this is measured — the pipeline

Two independent paths, both auditable: passive registry stats (no client code at all) and the opt-in census (strict validation at three layers). The datastore is a git branch — ingest history, rollup code, and every number here are public.

Freshness: census numbers re-roll within ~1 minute of any ping; registry numbers refresh nightly (PyPI publishes daily); badges re-poll every 5 minutes; this page fetches on load.

Why trust these numbers? They start honest (the savings counters began at zero — no estimates, no seeded totals), they can't be inflated (schema allowlists + hard numeric ceilings at the worker, in CI, and again at rollup; one census per machine, latest wins), and subscription users' notional dollars are excluded from the $ total. Full schema and kill switches: TELEMETRY.md.
